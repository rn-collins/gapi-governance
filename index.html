About

The Governance Gap in AI Is Not About Capability.

The governance gap in AI is not about what agents can do. It is about what institutional frameworks exist to assess whether what they do is cognitively safe, ethically sound, and aligned with the nervous system states of the humans they interact with.

GAPI applies the NSAG framework to agentic AI systems. Fourteen dimensions. Scored. Certified. Integrated into your stack as an MCP server so governance runs at the infrastructure level — not as a quarterly audit that arrives after the harm.

This is the 0-to-1 play in AI governance. The market is defining what responsible agent behavior means. GAPI is the infrastructure that defines it in terms that are measurable, certifiable, and deployable.
